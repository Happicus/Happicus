Yahtzee Quick Rules

  • Objective: Score points by rolling 5 dice to make certain combinations across exactly 13 rounds.
  • Turn: Roll the dice up to 3 times. After each roll, you can hold any number of dice.
  • Scoring: Choose one category on the score sheet to fill in. If your dice don't match the requirements, you must "scratch" and take a 0.
  • Upper Bonus: Score 63 or more points in the upper section to get a 35-point bonus.

Qwixx Quick Rules

  • Objective: Score points by crossing out numbers from left to right.
  • The Golden Rule: Numbers must be crossed out from left to right. Once you cross out a number, you can never cross out any numbers to its left in that same row.
  • Turn: 1. Announce sum of white dice (anyone can cross). 2. Active player can combine 1 white + 1 colored die to cross in that color's row.
  • Locking: To cross the far right number (2 or 12), you must already have 5 crosses in that row. This automatically locks the row.
  • Penalties: If the active player crosses off zero numbers during their turn, they must take a penalty (-5 points).
